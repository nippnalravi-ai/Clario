About Clario

Clario was built with a simple goal: to give everyone access to a smart, reliable AI assistant that helps them think clearly and solve problems — without unnecessary complexity or barriers to entry.

We believe that clarity shouldn't be locked behind confusing interfaces or expensive subscriptions. Whether you're a student working through a tough assignment, a professional drafting an important email, or someone simply looking for a second opinion on an idea, Clario is designed to meet you where you are and help you move forward.

What Clario Offers

  • Conversational AI Chat — Ask questions, get explanations, brainstorm ideas, or work through complex problems step by step.
  • Image Generation — Turn your ideas and descriptions into visuals instantly.
  • Video Generation — Create short video clips directly from text prompts.
  • Flexible Plans — Start for free, or upgrade to Pro or Premium for higher usage limits, faster responses, and an ad-free experience.

Our Approach

Clario is built and maintained with a focus on simplicity, speed, and genuine usefulness. We're continually improving the app based on how people actually use it, with the goal of making it a dependable tool you can turn to whenever you need to think something through.

Have feedback, questions, or ideas? Visit our Contact page — we'd love to hear from you.

About Clario

Clario is a free AI assistant built to help you think things through — whether that's a tricky question, a decision you're stuck on, or an idea you want to explore. Type it in, and Clario helps you work it out.

Beyond conversation, Clario also generates images and video, so you can turn ideas into something visual without needing separate tools.

Clario is built and maintained independently by a solo developer, with a simple goal: make a genuinely useful AI tool that's free to start, easy to use, and doesn't get in your way.

Clario is under active development. Feedback — good or critical — genuinely shapes what gets built next. If something's confusing, broken, or missing, reach out.
